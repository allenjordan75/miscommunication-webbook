IPC 2110 Personal Chapter Drafting Template

Use this template to plan a full textbook chapter that begins with an opening story, moves from common sense as knowing toward (un)common sense as understanding, and develops three discontinuous leaps supported by sources, quotes, callouts, illustrations, and endnote-ready citations.

How to use this template

  1. Fill the chapter-level setup first, including the voice anchor, the big shift, and the Common Sense / (Un)Common Sense framework for the topic.
  2. For each chapter section, complete the framing, idea development, sources, bibliography, direct quotes, callout ideas, illustration ideas, and help request boxes.
  3. Use the master AI prompt when you want ChatGPT to draft the full chapter at once, or use the section prompts when you want to work one section at a time.
  4. Use the export buttons to create a Word, HTML, text, or Markdown planning file.

Chapter flow

Chapter Architecture

Use this as the default chapter order: Opening Story -> Overview -> Approaches -> Discontinuous Leaps -> Practice -> Closing.

1

Achieve curiosity, relevance, emotional stakes, and a concrete problem that students can recognize before concepts appear.

Section goal: define, ground, and prepare.

3

H1: Approaching [Topic]

Contrast common sense as knowing with (un)common sense as understanding through definitions and operating narratives.

Section goal: compare approaches without doing the leaps yet.

4

H1: Discontinuous Leaps

Develop three assumption shifts that change what students notice, believe, and do.

Section goal: transform assumptions into behavioral consequences.

5

H1: Practice: Rules to Heuristics

Bridge one familiar common sense skill into a more flexible (un)common sense skill.

Section goal: make the conceptual shift practicable.

6

Closing: What to Carry Forward

Return to the opening story and consolidate what students can now notice, understand, and try.

Section goal: synthesize, transfer, and motivate continued practice.

Quality control

Final Chapter Checks

Use this list before drafting, revising, or asking ChatGPT to review the chapter.

  • The chapter begins with the opening story, not a front-matter overview.
  • Every section has a fixed section goal that stays consistent across chapters.
  • Overview and Practice use a section claim plus expandable main idea boxes with evidence/development and callout ideas.
  • Every section keeps direct quotes and a specific help request box.
  • Every AI prompt focuses on goals, outcomes, rhetorical moves, transitions, repetition checks, endnotes, voice, callouts, and illustrations.
  • The two approach overview boxes define and narrate common sense and (un)common sense before the leaps begin.
  • There are exactly three discontinuous leaps.
  • Each leap includes common sense assumption, myth, communication impact, and potential examples.
  • Each leap includes (un)common sense assumption, belief, and behavior change with examples.
  • The Practice section bridges one common sense skill to one (un)common sense skill through a small example narrative.
  • The comparative illustration appears near the end and draws from the leaps with accessibility guidance for screen readers and text tools.
  • The prompt pack at the bottom lets selected prompts run together and includes the voice anchor.
  • Drafted citations appear as endnotes.
